About one hour with us up front, then a few minutes a week approving matches. That's the whole time commitment.

What a profile makeover looks like

Most profiles fail on the bio and prompts, not the person. Here's the kind of rewrite every client gets.

Before

"Work hard, play hard. Love to travel, good food and good vibes. Looking for someone who doesn't take life too seriously."

  • Could describe almost anyone
  • Gives matches nothing to reply to
  • No hint of what you actually want

After

"Product lead by day, amateur pasta maker by Sunday. Last trip: Lisbon, mostly for the custard tarts. Looking for someone who'd rather plan the next trip than talk about the last one."

  • Specific details people remember
  • Two easy openers built in: pasta and Lisbon
  • Signals someone looking for something real

Frequently asked questions

What is a dating app management service?

It's a team that runs your online dating for you. A dating app management service handles your profile, photos, swiping, messaging and date scheduling, so you only spend time on the dates themselves.

Is a virtual dating assistant worth it?

If your time is worth more than the hours dating apps take each week, usually yes. Clients come to us to get better matches, fewer dead-end chats and dates that actually happen, without giving up their evenings to their phone.

Do you only write dating profiles?

No. Our Profile Reset plan is a one-time dating profile makeover with photo selection, bio and prompts. Full Service, Concierge and Elite add daily swiping, messaging and date booking.

Who do you work with?

Busy professionals who are serious about finding a relationship. We work with singles of every gender and orientation.

Do you message matches as me?

Yes, on Full Service, Concierge and Elite. Your manager writes in your voice, based on your onboarding call. You can read every conversation, take over whenever you like, and nothing is booked until you approve it.

Which apps do you manage?

Hinge, Bumble, Tinder, Coffee Meets Bagel and The League. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and city on the first call.

How do you keep my information private?

Every client signs a mutual NDA. Only your assigned manager can access your accounts, logins are kept in a secure password manager, and your photos and notes are deleted when you leave.

How soon will I go on a date?

Most clients see new matches in the first week, and first dates usually follow within a few weeks. It depends on your city, criteria and availability, and we'll set expectations on the first call.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. Plans renew monthly. Pause or cancel anytime before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
